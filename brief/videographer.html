Videographer Brief · The Vibe
01

What we're about

We put on a genuine rock show. Four to six tech-industry bands, real instruments, a real crowd, and a room that gets loud. Our job with video is to make the people on that stage feel like rock stars — and to give the crowd a night they want to relive.

Real BOTTB, real stakes.  The Triffid, Brisbane — this is the energy we want on camera.

Your creativity leads. We are not after sterile corporate coverage. We want originality, a point of view, and a signature. Get on stage, get into the thick of the crowd, chase the light and the energy.

We're a charity, and honest about it. We keep costs low and are sometimes limited by what sponsorship provides. That's exactly why we offer a menu of options — pitch what fits your kit and your rate.

We back local artists. Supporting up-and-coming photographers and videographers is part of why this event exists. We promote the people who shoot it — see page 8.

We shoot for reuse. Footage promotes future events, so timeliness and technical reliability genuinely matter. Where we're specific, it's from experience — not to box in your craft.

05

Scope & pricing

Please quote each line separately. Listed roughly in order of priority — take on what suits your kit and rate.

#
Service
1
Footage only Required
Multi-camera capture of the full show — four angles (wide, two angled, one roving), run by a small crew: one roving operator plus a producer/operator on the fixed cameras. Full technical spec on page 6.
2
Reels & highlights Add-on
Six reels — one event overview plus one per band. Backed by the studio original of the song the band covered (e.g. a band covering Rage Against the Machine → the RATM original), not the live mix — kept to ≤60s for fair use, so they're never blocked on our audio. Horizontal source, vertical-safe framing.
3
Multitrack audio Add-on
The Triffid's desk has no multitrack outs, so the incoming feeds need to be split (a splitter/snake on the inputs) into a recorder — handed off as .wav files. A dedicated tech to monitor the record is optional. Record only; we mix and master in-house, and we'll supply input lists per act a few days out.
4
Full edited show If available
The complete show edited from multicam, including grading — delivered as an editor project plus a final rendered master (see note below).
Editing tools — Premiere or Resolve

We're glad to take either — cut in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve. Deliver a standard interchange (FCPXML / Premiere XML / AAF / EDL) that relinks cleanly, alongside the graded media and the final rendered master. We archive in Resolve, so a Resolve project is welcome — but never required.

Videographer Brief · Technical Requirements
07

Footage — the shots

Everything filmed horizontally, with framing mindful of vertical reuse. Extra angles are always a bonus.

1 · Wide — full stage
  • Ideally 6K to maximise post digital zoom
  • Locked and stable; covers the whole stage for the full show
2 · Two angled cameras
  • Shooting towards the stage, typically from the left and right
  • Tighter performer coverage to intercut with the wide
3 · One roving camera
  • Roams the photo pit between stage and barricade — free to spin back for crowd reactions
  • Up on stage for a few grabs each set for the reels — get amongst the action
+ Bonus angles
  • Slider or behind-the-band, crowd-facing angles are gold
  • Any additional angles welcome — surprise us, originality is the point
08

Delivery & capture spec

Formats & media
  • Raw footage minimum 4K, in Log or Blackmagic RAW
  • 24 fps base rate — set shutter to avoid flicker under stage lighting (25 fps is fine); higher rates welcome for slow-mo B-roll
  • Delivered on an SSD we provide
  • Where a format lacks camera metadata, include camera make & model
Audio
  • All video must include audio for syncing — set sensible levels
  • Taking the multitrack line (page 5)? You capture the desk split; we'll send input lists a few days out
  • We also run our own board + mic recording (Zoom) as backup and to mix in a little crowd
Reliability & consistency on the night
  • Record to dual cards / backup where your camera supports it
  • Keep white balance & picture profile matched across cameras for cleaner multicam grading
  • Mind camera vibration in your setup and mounting
  • Check cameras between sets for overheating / shut-off
  • Confirm cards are recording and levels are healthy after each changeover
  • Have a redundancy plan for the winner reveal and key moments

10

Ownership & usage

We own the work — you're free to show it

You're paid to create it, so BOTTB owns the footage and edits — and we keep that fair:

  • Use your own work, always — feature anything you shoot for us in your portfolio and showreel, crediting BOTTB.
  • Published under CC BY-NC 4.0 — like all media on our site, released for non-commercial use with attribution to you, so it gets seen and shared.
  • Commercial use stays with us — and if a paid or commercial opportunity for the work comes up, we'll come to you first.
11

Delivery & payment

Flexible on dates, simple on payment
  • Delivery dates are negotiable — the dates above are what we'd love, agreed with you up-front. A heads-up keeps everything flexible and we can almost always shuffle a date.
  • No penalty clauses baked in — we'd rather get the timing right together than dock a fee. Anything that waits on us (audio, a hero song, an asset we owe you) never counts against your timeline.
  • 50% deposit to lock the date, balance on delivery; edited finals are released once payment clears. Standard, and negotiable.
