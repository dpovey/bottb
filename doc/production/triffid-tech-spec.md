## Access, load-in and parking

- **Venue access from 3pm on show day.** The Venue Production Manager can approve earlier access on request; this may incur additional charges.

## What this means for BOTTB

Five bands in a room specced for one headliner plus supports. The pack confirms several things we already work around, and settles a few open questions:

- **Multitrack is not native.** The FOH desk is an Avid SC48 and the pack advertises no recording path — consistent with the videographer brief, which specifies splitting the inputs into a separate recorder and handing off .wav files. Budget for a splitter/snake and a recordist; don't expect a desk capture.
- **One green room for five bands.** Single band room, so the rotation plan stands. The mitigation is in the spec: the room has private access to the beer garden and bistro, so overflow has somewhere to go that isn't the floor.
- **Backdrop is projection, not LED.** 200" 16:9 at 1920×1080 over HDMI — per-band visuals must be authored to 1080p 16:9 and driven from a laptop at FOH/LX (15 m from DSE).
- **Rolling changeovers are viable.** Two 300 mm risers (8'×6' and 8'×5') support a drum swap, but wing space is tight — 2 m² USL and 2 m × 3 m USR won't hold five bands' backline. Stage-side storage needs to be advanced explicitly.
- **Load-in is the easy part.** 30 m flat from a loading zone at the front door, no stairs, plus one touring-vehicle space. Compare the Factory Theatre's 30 stairs when the lift isn't arranged.
- **3pm access is the binding constraint.** Doors at ~6:30pm leaves roughly 3.5 hours for a five-band load-in, line check and soundcheck. Early access needs PM approval and costs extra — price it in rather than discovering it on the day.
- **Capacity headroom.** 800 standing including guests against a typical 400–500 draw: comfortable, and the room still reads full.
- **House backline covers the basics only.** Two guitar heads, two cabs, one bass rig, one kit. Five bands will bring their own; the house kit is a backup, not a plan.
