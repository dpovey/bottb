Two sleeps until Battle of the Tech Bands Sydney, so here's one more from Brisbane.

Epsonics, the band from Epsilon, playing Queens of the Stone Age's "No One Knows" at The Triffid in Brisbane. Starting off strong, the Epsonics opened their eclectic set with this early 2000s banger. With gritty vocals, growling guitars and its fast paced and urgent drums it helped set the scene for this performance which took the highest judges' score of the night and finished second overall.

Sydney is Thursday 8 October at Manning Bar, with bands from Atlassian, Canva, Amazon and V2 AI, and Brisbane champions ShipReX as special guests.
