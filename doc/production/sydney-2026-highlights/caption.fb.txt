What an amazing night! It was great to be back in Sydney for a second year, seeing many familiar faces and some new ones.

Congratulations again to The Canvanauts from Canva on the win. The standard was incredibly high, and in a field like that it was always going to be close. Amakazaam! from Amazon played a tight, energetic set to take second place and the judges' vote. V2 Voyagers from V2 AI took us on a journey through space to third place and best Crowd Vibe. And who can forget Bandlassian from Atlassian and their Pokéball-bouncing finale!

Special thanks to Brisbane winners ShipReX from Rex Software and URBAN X, who trekked down to uncharted waters to open the Sydney night with their trademark energy and piratey zeal.

Raffles and merch raised around $2,700 for Youngcare, with the door take still to come.
