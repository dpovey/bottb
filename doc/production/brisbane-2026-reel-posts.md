## Voice rules used for this draft

From the research (Julie Holmes' trope list, MagicPost's 11 patterns, Team Lewis, Flick): no em
dashes, no "the moment when", no rule-of-three lists, no "it's not X, it's Y", no emoji at both
ends, no "quietly", no hedging, no summary sentence at the end. First person plural (we ran the
night). Vary sentence length. Lead with a specific detail someone in the room would recognise.
Emphasis on the community, the energy in the room, and how good the playing actually was.

## Schedule (AEST)

Research: LinkedIn is strongest Tue to Thu, late afternoon into early evening. Instagram and
Facebook in Australia peak 5 to 10 pm Monday to Thursday. TikTok in Australia peaks 7 to 10 pm,
and you want to post 30 to 60 minutes ahead of that. YouTube Shorts do best published mid to late
afternoon. A single late-afternoon burst per day lands inside all four windows and gives the
band one moment to rally around.

| Day        | Reel             | LinkedIn | YouTube  | IG + FB  | TikTok   |

| Mon 31 Aug | Night highlights | 4:30 pm  | 5:00 pm  | 6:00 pm  | 6:30 pm  |

Friday is fine for Instagram and TikTok, softer for LinkedIn. Saturday late morning is the best
weekend window on IG/TikTok; LinkedIn is quiet on weekends, so the band sharing it matters more
that day.

How each gets posted: Facebook and Instagram through the Graph API (FB accepts a scheduled time;
IG has to be published live, so I'll run it from a scheduled job). LinkedIn: composer's "Schedule
post" via Chrome. YouTube: Studio schedule via Chrome (our API key is read-only). TikTok: web
upload via Chrome, schedules up to 10 days out.
