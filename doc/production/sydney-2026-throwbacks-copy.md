**Fri 2 Oct, Bandlassian "Rosanna"** (`Atlassian2026`)

Six days until Battle of the Tech Bands Sydney, and Bandlassian are back to defend their crowd vote.

Last year Atlassian's band opened with two Toto songs back to back. This is the second, Rosanna,
from the Factory Theatre in October 2025. They brought the biggest crew of the night and took the
crowd vote. See what they've got for Manning Bar on Thursday 8 October.

**Sat 3 Oct, Jamazon "Alexa introduces Jamazon"** (`Amazon2026`)

Last year Amazon's band got their introduction from Alexa. This year they're back with a new name:
Amakazaam!

As Jamazon they finished runners-up at Sydney 2025, with mash-ups, multimedia backdrops and
confetti cannons, on a set they put together in four weeks. Five days until they take the Manning
Bar stage.

**Sun 4 Oct, Canvanauts backstage** (`Canva2026`)

The Canvanauts came within five votes of winning last year's crowd vote. Eddy Hill caught up with
Canva's band backstage at Sydney 2025, after a set that ran from Jet and Amy Winehouse to Elton
John, Queen and a heavy take on Taylor Swift's Anti-Hero. Four days until they're back, this time
at Manning Bar.

**Mon 5 Oct, Bandlassian backstage** (`Atlassian2026`)

Backstage with Bandlassian at Sydney 2025, straight after the set that won them the crowd vote and
third place overall. Interview by Eddy Hill. Three days until Atlassian's band are back on stage.
