# Battle of the Tech Bands Sydney 2026 — venue tech pack

Thursday 8 October 2026 · Manning Bar, University of Sydney · doors 6:30 pm

Five bands, sets of about 20–25 minutes each. **Running order to be confirmed**, so bands are listed below in no
particular order.

## 1. Recording

We're recording audio and filming video of the whole event. For the audio, we'd like to record every input, taken **before any desk processing**, plus the stereo mix, over the desk's UB MADI
USB port (up to 48 channels). We bring the recording laptop; we just need the USB connection at FOH and space for
a laptop.

- **Tracks 1–32:** every input in the patch below, taken after the preamp and before EQ, dynamics, faders and
  mutes (direct outs at the earliest tap point, or the input patched straight to UB MADI).
- **Tracks 33–34:** the stereo main mix (post-fader L/R, or a matrix copy of it).
- 48 kHz / 24-bit. We'll test it at soundcheck, then record continuously from the opening intro to the end of
  the night. One of our team will check on the laptop periodically.
- Because the recording follows the preamps, **please set gains at soundcheck and avoid changing them during a
  set**. Fader moves are fine; they don't touch the multitrack.

**Backup stereo mix:** please also send the stereo mix to **two of the console's rear XLR line outputs**. We'll
record them on our own Zoom recorder at FOH, taking them in at line level (XLR-female to TRS cables into the
Zoom's jack inputs, not its mic preamps). The Zoom may also record the room from FOH.

**Could you supply two XLR-female to TRS (¼" jack) cables?** The console's line outputs run at line level. The
Zoom's XLR sockets go through its mic preamps, which would boost that signal and distort it, while its TRS jack
inputs take line level cleanly.

## 4. Questions for the venue

1. **In-ear feeds:** we need three: one wired XLR feed (Bandlassian's drummer) and two for bands' own wireless
   transmitters (Amakazaam! and V2 Voyagers' lead singers).
2. **Boomless stand:** do you have a straight, boomless mic stand for Amakazaam!'s lead singer? It holds her
   wireless mic and clip at centre stage (Vox 2 position).
3. **Unused mics:** can the crew fold down or move the vocal mics a band isn't using before each set? We're filming
   and photographing every set, and clear sightlines to the players make a big difference.
4. **HDMI at the drum riser:** can we get an HDMI run from the riser to the screen, as well as side stage?
5. **Cables:** can you supply two XLR-female to TRS cables for the backup recorder (see section 1)?
