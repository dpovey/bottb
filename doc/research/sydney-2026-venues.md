# Battle of the Tech Bands: Sydney 2026 Venue Survey

**Prepared:** July 2026
**Target:** October 2026, weeknight (likely Thursday), 5 bands
**Expected attendance:** 500–650 (Sydney 2025 drew ~450 at Factory Theatre; growth trajectory assumed)
**Requirements:** Rock-show feel (live music venues only), professional PA/lighting, FOH console with multitrack recording, projector/LED screen for band backdrops, green room + gear storage for 5 bands, filming positions. Backline is hireable — nice-to-have only.

Research method: 3 parallel discovery sweeps (gig listings, venue-hire directories, Reddit/music press) followed by an independent deep-dive research agent per venue, covering official tech-spec packs, reviews (Google/Yelp/TripAdvisor/Reddit), and photo evidence. All photo URLs verified to serve images. All info should be re-verified with venues before booking.

### 1. Factory Theatre — Main Room (800 GA) — fit 8.5/10

![BOTTB Sydney 2025 at Factory Theatre](https://0qipqwe5exqqyona.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/photos/7139434a-0a60-4736-a58f-5bab569edcc3/large.webp)
![Band backdrop projection in use, BOTTB 2025](https://0qipqwe5exqqyona.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/photos/a5adf21c-e441-4494-bf14-6680ee010cb2/large.webp)
![Live at the Factory (Backseat Mafia, 2023)](https://www.backseatmafia.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/DSC_6381.jpg)

**Address:** 105 Victoria Road, Marrickville · **Operator:** Century Venues
**Booking:** boxoffice@century.com.au · (02) 9550 3666 · hire form at factorytheatre.com.au/hire/

The incumbent — BOTTB Sydney 2025 ran here (Thu 23 Oct 2025, six bands, ~450 attendance). Purpose-built black-box rock room in a converted paper factory: raised 1.13 m stage, industrial trussing, festoon-lit outdoor courtyard with permanent food truck — the courtyard is the closest Sydney analogue to The Triffid's beer garden.

| Component    | Details                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Capacity     | 800 GA / 600 theatre / 300 cabaret (some third-party listings wrongly say 450)                                                             |
| FOH console  | Allen & Heath dLive S5000 + DM48 (upgraded from Yamaha M7CL, 2023→2024); monitors dLive S3500                                              |
| Multitrack   | Likely via Dante (DM48 is a 48×48 Dante/MADI bridge) — bring capture laptop; no turnkey service advertised                                 |
| PA           | NEXO GEO S1210 line array (flown), RS-series subs                                                                                          |
| Lighting     | GrandMA3 Command Wing; Acme moving spots/pars, LED profiles, blinders — touring-grade                                                      |
| **Backdrop** | **Motorised 3.5 m × 4 m cinema screen built into stage — confirmed in use for band logos at BOTTB 2025 (photo above)**                     |
| Green room   | **1 dressing room only, 7.6 m × 2.8 m (~21 m²)** + kitchenette — tight for 5 bands; plan rotation + courtyard overflow                     |
| Gear storage | Undocumented — side-of-stage/wings by arrangement; house rules require passages kept clear                                                 |
| Stage        | 8 m × 6 m, 1.13 m high; 2.4 m beartrap drum riser; in-house riggers only                                                                   |
| Load-in      | Courtyard gates; lift by arrangement with BOH Manager, else 30 stairs; trucks can't park out front until 1 hr post-show                    |
| Filming      | No mezzanine/balcony — flat floor; FOH position 16 m back at floor level; camera riser would eat floor capacity                            |
| Crew         | House audio + lighting kit included in hire; techs quoted per event (budget ~$400–450+GST/10 hr each)                                      |
| Curfew       | Not published; Thursday shows proven (BOTTB 2025 ran doors 6:30pm)                                                                         |
| Transport    | Buses 423/426 at door (run past midnight); Newtown/Sydenham/Marrickville stations ~15–20 min walk                                          |
| Reviews      | ~4.5 Google (aggregator, unverified) / 4.0 Yelp; praise for sound + raised-stage sightlines; gripes: $3 cloakroom fee, industrial location |

> "The concert hall is spacious and the sound quality was excellent." — TripAdvisor
> "The venue is quirky with festival vibes… a real buzz in the grounds with heated outside bar area." — TripAdvisor

**Verdict:** Everything this event needs is proven here, in production, at this exact event. The two real weaknesses — a single small dressing room and no elevated filming position — are known quantities you already worked around in 2025. The question is whether Liberty Hall's better backstage and filming justify giving up a proven room.

## Tier 2: Detailed Analysis

### 4. UNSW Roundhouse — Kensington (1,250 main room, curtainable) — fit 7/10

![Roundhouse gig (Arc UNSW)](https://www.arc.unsw.edu.au/generated/hero-large/rh236658-roundhouse-website-hero-gigs-and-concerts-png.png?1732247644)

**Booking:** roundhouse@arc.unsw.edu.au · (02) 9385 7630

Sydney's only circular band room (1961), fully redeveloped ~2021–23. Deep pedigree: Foo Fighters, Green Day, INXS, Nick Cave, Fontaines D.C.

- **Production is the best documented of any candidate:** DiGiCo SD12 FOH (onboard UB-MADI USB multitrack, 48ch) + SD8 monitors; d&b V-Series flown array (6×Vi8 + 2×Vi12 per side, 6×B22 subs); High End Hog 4 + LSC Clarity driving Martin/ETC/Chauvet rig on front, rear and signature circular truss.
- **Scalability is designed-in:** a fire-curtain track runs the room specifically to shrink it for smaller shows — the right ask is "main room curtained to ~800–900."
- Video: two mid-tier Epson projectors + Roland vision switcher — workable for logos, not an LED-wall showpiece.
- Backstage: one green room (private shower/toilet) + adjacent dedicated storage rooms on the floor plan; overflow via Level 1 function rooms by negotiation.
- Mezzanine balcony marketed for "amazing sightlines over the crowd and main stage" — good camera position.
- UNSW light rail stop directly outside; express bus from Central.
- Unknowns: pricing (quote-only, corporate rate on a uni venue), load-in specifics, Ticketek default ticketing.

**Verdict:** Beats everything on documented PA/lighting and matches the multitrack requirement, but you'd be actively managing the room size down, and backstage is a single green room. Strong fallback if Tier 1 rooms fall through on dates.

### 5. Metro Theatre — CBD (1,100) — fit 6.5/10

![Wednesday at the Metro, June 2026 (Backseat Mafia)](https://www.backseatmafia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/wednesday-Deb-Pelser-2200-1160x934.jpg)

**Booking:** Priava form via metrotheatre.com.au/hire-the-metro/ · (02) 7208 4483 · Century Venues (same operator as Factory Theatre)

Sydney's most storied mid-size rock room (Jeff Buckley 1995, You Am I's seven straight sell-outs, Coldplay, Muse, Lorde).

- **Confirmed from the official Aug 2025 tech-spec PDF:** A&H dLive S5000 FOH with **both Dante and Waves cards** (strongest verified multitrack after Manning Bar); L-Acoustics V-DOSC hangs + SB218 subs; GrandMA3 rig; **permanent 4.5 m × 2.5 m LED screen (3.9 mm pitch) on the upstage truss with HDMI input at stage right or FOH** — the best backdrop infrastructure of any candidate.
- Two dressing rooms + production office (showers in both) — workable with rotation.
- House audio + lighting included in base hire; early/late access $120+GST/hr.
- **Deal-breakers to weigh:** ~1,100 cap reads half-empty at 550; and the loading dock path is **7 m to a staircase then 29 stairs to stage with no goods lift** — a real problem for five bands' worth of changeover gear.
- Google 4.3 (953); 3 min walk from Town Hall Station.

**Verdict:** On pure spec this is the best room on the list; for _this_ event at _this_ attendance, the size and the stairs relegate it. Reconsider if attendance projections jump.

### 6. Selina's — Coogee Bay Hotel (~734) — fit 5.5/10

![Mallrat at Selina's (venue site)](https://selinas.com.au/content/uploads/2023/03/SELINAS_MALLRAT_PRINT-29-1920x1536.jpg)

**Booking:** bookings@selinas.com.au

The most storied pedigree in Sydney — Bowie, Nirvana, Radiohead, Foo Fighters, INXS, Cold Chisel all played this room. Renovated 2023–24 with "concert-spec PA from Allen & Heath, Outline, EAW & JBL," new truss/lighting, and a **giant mobile rear-of-stage LED screen**. Mezzanine gives a filming position (with some obstructed-view complaints).

**The problem:** the Coogee Bay Hotel site has planning approval (May 2024) for a redevelopment that **demolishes the building housing Selina's**, and the owner is on record that Selina's won't reopen afterwards. It's still trading with a dense 2026 calendar and no announced closure date, but booking an October 2026 charity event into a room with an approved demolition overhead is a real risk. Console, multitrack, green rooms (historically informal balcony space) and load-in are all undocumented on top of that.

**Verdict:** Only pursue if the venue can contractually guarantee the date. Would be a memorable "last dance" story if timing works — and a cancellation nightmare if it doesn't.

## Tier 3

### 7. Oxford Art Factory — Darlinghurst (500) — fit 5/10

![OAF Main Room (Time Out)](https://media.timeout.com/images/103023695/1920/1080/image.jpg)

**Booking:** Millie Rose Lee millie@oxfordartfactory.com · Production: Steve Coull production@oxfordartfactory.com

Astonishing production for a 500-cap room — d&b Yi line array, DiGiCo SD12 with Waves card (bring a SoundGrid server to record), GrandMA2, motorised 16:9 projection screen with HDMI from FOH (3,000-lumen projector — may wash out under full stage lighting). Published, reasonable tech rates (audio $400+GST/8 hr, lighting $300+GST/6 hr) and the most transparent contacts of any venue.

But: 500 cap sells out at expected attendance and caps the growth story; **one** shared dressing room; stage is 4.8 m × 4.2 m with a single 1.2 m wing (genuinely tight for 5-band changeovers); rear-lane load-out banned after 8pm ($1,000 council fine).

**Verdict:** Wrong size and wrong logistics for this format, despite superb per-head production quality.

## Venue Enquiry Template

Things web research can't answer — send to each shortlisted venue:

> Subject: Venue hire enquiry — Battle of the Tech Bands, October 2026 (charity event, ~550 pax)
>
> Hi [name],
>
> We run Battle of the Tech Bands, a corporate battle-of-the-bands charity event supporting Youngcare (battleofthetechbands.com). Five bands from major tech companies play short sets to a corporate crowd of 500–650. Our 2025 Sydney event ran at the Factory Theatre; Brisbane runs at The Triffid. We're looking at a weeknight (likely Thursday) in October 2026.
>
> Could you let us know:
>
> 1. **Availability** — open Thursdays in October 2026, and what a hold looks like
> 2. **Hire terms** — room hire, minimum spend, what's included; any charity/community event pricing
> 3. **Crew** — which techs are included vs charged (rates), early-access/bump-in fees
> 4. **Console & recording** — FOH desk model, and whether we can capture a multitrack of the night (Dante/MADI/USB); can your engineer support this?
> 5. **Backdrops** — projector/LED screen behind stage for per-band visuals; size and input (HDMI/SDI)
> 6. **Backstage** — green room/dressing rooms available, and where 5 bands' gear can be stored during the show
> 7. **Changeovers** — load-in access and whether a rolling backline changeover (drum riser swap) works in your wings
> 8. **Filming** — elevated camera positions, and any filming approval requirements
> 9. **Sponsors** — can we place pull-up banners / sponsor activations; any restrictions
> 10. **Ticketing** — must we use your platform, or can we sell through our own (we use Moshtix in Brisbane)
> 11. **Security & insurance** — what's included; your public liability requirements for hirers
> 12. Position for a judges' table with clear sightlines, and floor space for a merch stand
>
> Happy to jump on a call. Thanks!
